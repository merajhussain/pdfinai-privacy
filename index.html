PdfInAI Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026 · App: PdfInAI (com.inpdf.pdftoolkit) · Developer: AMBS

This policy explains how the PdfInAI Android app ("the app") handles your information. In short: your documents stay on your device, we do not run any servers that receive your data, and we do not use ads, analytics or tracking.

1. Information we (AMBS) collect

We do not collect, store or receive your documents, images, text, API keys or any personal information. The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting services.

2. Files and images you open

PDFs and images you choose are processed on your device to display, create, edit, split or merge PDFs. The app keeps temporary working copies in its private cache, which are deleted when you leave the screen or by Android. Saved PDFs go only to the location you choose. The app uses the Android file and photo pickers, so it only accesses the files you pick.

3. Optional AI features

AI features are off until you turn them on in AI settings and enter your own API key for a provider you choose (an OpenAI-compatible service, Anthropic or Google Gemini). When you use an AI feature:

  • The app sends your request directly from your device to that provider over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. For text this is your instruction and, when you ask for a rewrite, the text you want rewritten. For images it is your description.
  • The data is not sent to AMBS. It is handled by the provider under their own terms and privacy policy, which you accepted when you created your API key.
  • Your API keys are stored only on your device, encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore. They are sent only to the provider you configured. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Do not send confidential or personal information to an AI provider unless you are comfortable with that provider's data practices.

4. Reporting AI content

If you use Report AI content, the app opens your email app with a pre-filled message containing your selected reason, your optional comment, the AI request and the result, the AI model name, and the app and Android versions. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send that email. We use reports only to review and improve how AI content is handled, and delete them once they are resolved.

5. Permissions

PermissionWhy
InternetOnly to contact the AI provider you configured, when you use an AI feature.

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or all of your files or photos.

6. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell or share your data. Data reaches a third party only when you use an AI feature, and then only the AI provider you chose, at your request.

7. Data retention and deletion

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Data stored by the app on your device (settings, encrypted API keys, temporary files) is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. For data held by your AI provider, please use that provider's tools or contact them.

8. Security

Connections to AI providers use HTTPS. API keys are encrypted at rest using the Android Keystore.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and is intended for a general adult audience.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date at the top shows when it was last updated.
